About

A small calculator with visible rules.

What it calculates

GradePath multiplies each recognised grade point by its subject credits, adds those weighted points, and divides by the included credits. CGPA applies the same calculation across every semester.

What it stores

The selected scale, custom scales, semesters, subject names, credits, and grades can be stored in local storage on this device. There is no account or cloud sync. If storage is blocked or full, calculations continue for the current page session.

Limits

Presets can become outdated and institutions may use different rules for exclusions, repeats, or rounding. Always verify the current official grading scheme and academic record.
