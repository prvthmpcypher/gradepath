Formulas and working notes

SGPA

SGPA = Σ(credit × grade point) ÷ Σ(credit)

Incomplete rows, zero or negative credits, and grades missing from the selected scale are not included.

CGPA

CGPA = Σ(all included weighted points) ÷ Σ(all included credits)

This is credit weighted across semesters; it is not a simple average of displayed SGPAs.

Custom scales

Labels must be unique, one to five characters long, and use points from 0 to 10. GradePath rejects duplicate labels and incomplete rows.

Display and saving

Results display two decimal places. Full numeric values are used in the calculation before display rounding. Saved browser data is validated and bounded when it is loaded.

Check your institution’s current official grading scheme. GradePath cannot account for every academic policy.
